# Reception pathways — slim-down proposal

**What this is:** a design proposal, not a content change. The v1.9
bundled set stays CSO-signed. Constraint: a **call** taps out at **10
spoken questions**, even if that means amalgamating.

**Verdict:** keep every red-flag meaning; ask them as two same-tier
lists (emergency / duty today), not a yes/no row each; two history
turns per condition; stop the line on a 999. Do not build a 111-style
wizard.

Every call also has the same wrap, counted inside the ten, not written
out per condition below: who you are speaking to, age on this call,
what’s the problem in their words, how long and is it better or worse,
what they were hoping for, best number. What follows is only the
**individual questions for each condition**.

A list read as “any of these?” is one question. Empty is not “all no”
— they tick **None of these**. A 999 yes generates now; later questions
stamp not asked.

---

## Condition questions

### Sore throat (3)

1. Any of these — we stop and treat as an emergency: difficulty
   breathing or noisy breathing; drooling or unable to swallow saliva;
   a rash that does not fade when a glass is pressed; if a child —
   floppy, unusually drowsy, or not drinking at all?
2. Any of these — duty doctor today: unable to open the mouth, or a
   muffled “hot potato” voice; severe pain on one side of the throat
   with swelling of the face or neck; medicines that weaken the immune
   system (carbimazole, methotrexate, chemotherapy, and the like)?
3. Any fever in the last 24 hours, and are they managing to eat and
   drink?

### Earache (4)

1. Any of these — emergency: redness or swelling behind the ear, or
   the ear sticking out more than usual; severe headache with a stiff
   neck, dislike of light, or a rash that does not fade with a glass;
   if a child — floppy, hard to wake, breathing very fast, or not
   drinking?
2. Any of these — duty today: a recent head injury, or fluid leaking
   from the ear after a bang to the head; sudden complete hearing loss
   in that ear; drooping or weakness on that side of the face?
3. Which ear, and is there any discharge?
4. Any fever?

### Cough / chest, adult (3)

1. Any of these — emergency: so breathless they cannot finish a
   sentence, or breathless at rest; blue or grey lips or face;
   coughing up a large amount of blood, or blood with breathlessness
   or chest pain; sharp pain on breathing in with a swollen calf,
   recent surgery, or a long flight; severe chest pain with
   breathlessness or sweating; new confusion or unusual drowsiness?
2. Any of these — duty today: blood-streaking in the phlegm; if the
   cough has lasted more than 3 weeks — unexplained weight loss,
   drenching night sweats, or loss of appetite; known asthma or COPD
   and the reliever is hardly helping?
3. More breathless than usual on stairs or walking, and any known lung
   problems or regular inhalers?

### Urinary symptoms, woman 16–64 (4)

1. Any of these — emergency: fever or shivering with new confusion or
   so unwell they cannot keep fluids down; fever with shaking rigors;
   new confusion or unusual drowsiness (especially in an older
   person)?
2. Any of these — duty today: pain in the back or side at kidney
   level; vomiting and unable to keep fluids down; any chance she is
   pregnant? (If this is a man or a child under 16, leave this
   pathway.)
3. Which of these do they have: burning or stinging; going more often;
   sudden urgency; urine cloudy or smelly; blood in the urine; low
   tummy pain?
4. Any antibiotic allergies?

### Headache, adult (3)

1. Any of these — emergency: came on suddenly and worst within minutes
   (“thunderclap” / worst of their life); new weakness, slurred
   speech, vision loss, or one-sided face droop; fever with a stiff
   neck, dislike of light, or a non-fading rash; a head injury in the
   last couple of days with vomiting or drowsiness; a painful red eye
   or halos around lights; if they are over 50 — a brand-new headache
   with temple tenderness or pain on chewing, **and** new blurring,
   double vision, or sudden loss of vision; headache worst first thing
   or waking them, getting steadily worse, with unexplained vomiting;
   anyone else in the household with the same new headache (possible
   carbon monoxide)?
2. Any of these — duty today: if over 50 — that same new temple /
   chewing headache **without** visual symptoms; pregnant with a
   severe headache, swelling, or vision changes; on blood thinners
   with a new headache after any knock to the head?
3. Do they get headaches or migraines normally — is this the same or
   different?

### Low back pain, adult (3)

1. Any of these — emergency: new numbness or tingling around the
   genitals or back passage (saddle area); new trouble controlling
   bladder or bowels, difficulty starting, or unable to feel when
   passing urine; weakness or numbness in both legs?
2. Any of these — duty today: started after a significant fall or
   accident; fever or feeling very unwell with the back pain
   (especially if they inject drugs, have a weakened immune system, or
   recent back surgery); any history of cancer, and this is a new back
   pain?
3. Does the pain travel down a leg below the knee — no, one leg, or
   both?

### Facial pain / sinusitis (4)

1. Any of these — emergency: swelling around or behind an eye, the eye
   pushed forward, or a change in vision or eye movement; swelling or
   redness of the forehead; severe headache with a stiff neck,
   dislike of light, or a non-fading rash; new confusion, weakness on
   one side, or slurred speech?
2. Any of these — duty today: facial pain rapidly worsening with a
   very high fever and looking very unwell; medicines that weaken the
   immune system; blocked nose or discharge on **one** side only,
   especially yellow-green or bloody, not improving after a few weeks?
3. Any blocked or runny nose — what colour is the mucus?
4. Any fever?

### Feverish child (4)

1. Any of these — emergency: a rash that does not fade with a glass;
   floppy, very hard to wake, or an unusual cry; if under 18 months —
   a bulging or tense fontanelle when they are not crying; breathing
   very fast, grunting, or sucking in between the ribs; blue, grey,
   mottled, or very pale skin or lips; any fit or seizure; stiff neck
   or bothered by bright light?
2. Any of these — duty today: a baby under 3 months with any fever; no
   wet nappy (or not passing urine) for 12 hours, or sunken eyes?
3. Has the temperature been measured — what was it — and are they
   drinking and having wet nappies?
4. How are they in themselves — playing, clingy, sleepy?

### Rash / skin (4)

1. Any of these — emergency: the rash does **not** fade when a glass
   is pressed; swelling of the lips, tongue or face, or difficulty
   breathing with the rash; an area that is red, hot, very painful and
   spreading fast, **and** they feel very unwell; widespread
   blistering or peeling, including mouth, eyes or genitals, and
   feeling unwell; rash with severe headache, stiff neck, or dislike
   of light?
2. Any of these — duty today: started a new medicine, now a widespread
   rash and feeling unwell; a new or changing mole or pigmented mark?
3. Where is it, and is it spreading?
4. Any fever or feeling unwell with it?

### Something else / general (4)

1. Any of these — emergency: severe difficulty breathing right now;
   chest pain or pressure right now; face droop, arm weakness, or
   slurred speech; heavy bleeding that will not stop; collapsed, hard
   to wake, or new confusion; swelling of lips or tongue or struggling
   to breathe; shivering or shaking uncontrollably, mottled or clammy,
   or saying they might die; actively trying to harm themselves, or a
   plan and the means right now; an overdose, a serious injury, or
   someone else at risk; they have harmed themselves today and the
   injury needs emergency treatment; hearing, seeing, or believing
   things that are getting rapidly worse and they cannot cope?
2. Any of these — duty today: thoughts of harming themselves or
   someone else, but no immediate plan or means; blood in the urine in
   someone 45 or over without a urine infection (or coming back after
   treatment); a hoarse voice for 3 weeks or more without a cold?
3. What is the main thing worrying them about it?
4. How is it affecting day-to-day life?

### Urinary / genital, male (4)

1. Any of these — emergency: sudden severe pain in a testicle
   (especially in the last few hours, or with feeling sick); completely
   unable to pass urine, with a painful full feeling low in the tummy;
   fever with rigors, new confusion, or seriously unwell; a painful
   erection lasting more than four hours that will not go down?
2. Any of these — duty today: fever with pain in the back or side at
   kidney level; foreskin pulled back and stuck, with swelling or
   pain; visible blood in the urine; a lump or change in a testicle,
   even if it does not hurt?
3. Which part is the problem — waterworks, a testicle, both, or
   something else — and any burning when passing urine?
4. Any of: going more often, weak stream, trouble starting, up at
   night, sudden urgency, dribbling at the end?

### Gynae / urinary, female (4)

1. Any of these — emergency: any chance of pregnancy **and** severe or
   sudden pain on one side of the lower tummy, or faint or dizzy; any
   chance of pregnancy **and** pain in the tip of the shoulder; in
   early pregnancy — soaking a pad an hour, or faint, dizzy, or very
   unwell with the bleeding; sudden severe pain on one side with
   vomiting or feeling faint; fever with rigors, new confusion, or
   seriously unwell?
2. Any of these — duty today: in early pregnancy — bleeding or passing
   clots or tissue, **without** that heavy bleeding or faintness;
   vaginal bleeding after the menopause not explained by HRT; bleeding
   after sex more than once; bloating, feeling full very quickly, or
   loss of appetite on most days for three weeks or more (especially
   if 50 or over)?
3. Any bleeding at the moment — how does it compare with a normal
   period — and if there is pain, which side and how bad out of 10?
4. Any unusual vaginal discharge — colour, smell, itching?

### Mental health / emotional distress (5)

1. Any of these — emergency: actively trying to harm themselves right
   now, or a plan and the means immediately; an overdose, a serious
   injury, or someone else at risk; they have harmed themselves today
   **and** the injury needs emergency treatment (heavy bleeding, a
   deep wound, a burn); hearing, seeing, or believing things that are
   getting rapidly worse and they cannot cope?
2. Any of these — duty today: thoughts of harming themselves or
   someone else, but no immediate plan or means; they have harmed
   themselves today and the injury does **not** need emergency
   treatment; those unusual experiences are there but not rapidly
   worse; a child or vulnerable adult at risk of harm, neglect, or
   abuse; nowhere safe to be, or nobody with them while they are in
   distress?
3. Can you tell me what’s happening today? (Their own words — do not
   interpret.)
4. Is there anyone with you at the moment?
5. Are you already known to a mental health team or a crisis service?

On this pathway, question 3 replaces the generic “what’s the problem”
wrap. Do not also ask “are you safe” or “what were you hoping for” as
extra condition rows.

## What is not on the call

These stay available under “More for the clinician” (walk-in / quiet
desk), or drop as duplicates of a list above:

- Sore throat: cough, glands, earache, previous tonsillitis / quinsy.
- Earache: hearing change, grommets, painkillers tried.
- Cough: phlegm colour, wheeze, smoking, a second weight-loss line.
- Headache: location, score out of 10, photophobia, vomiting,
  painkiller days.
- Back pain: trigger, function, previous episodes.
- Sinusitis: toothache, previous sinusitis.
- Feverish child: immunisations, “other symptoms” multi, calpol
  (unless you want it fused into question 3).
- Rash: exposures, itch/pain, blisters, creams tried.
- Urinary: how many UTIs last year.
- Male GU: perineal pain, prostate / PSA; blood / fever / testicle as
  separate rows (already on the lists).
- Gynae: LMP / menopause; pregnancy / post-coital / ovarian / unwell
  as separate rows (already on the lists).
- Mental health: “are you safe”, “thoughts of harm”, “what were you
  hoping for” as extra rows.
- Every pathway: who they last saw; a second “tried so far”.

Child-only and over-50 lines only appear when age on this call makes
them possible. No confirmed age → ask them. Do not hide pregnancy,
blood-thinners, immunosuppression, or mental-health lines from the
open record.

## Guardrails

- Structured capture, not triage. All-no is not “safe to routine”.
- Do not delete a signed flag to hit ten. Amalgamate same tier; fuse
  history. Emergency and duty stay two questions, never one mixed
  “yes = maybe 999” blob.
- Do not mark leftover questions “no” to make Generate work.
- Mental health: no score, no destination, no “they sound low risk so
  skip the rest”.
- Male GU, gynae, mental health, and general stay clinician-only. No
  Pharmacy First suggestion as a side-effect of slimming.
- Custom packs use the same generate path and the same none-of-these
  rule.

## If we build it

1. **Renderer** — two lists + the condition questions above; Generate
   on an emergency yes; more-drawer for the leftovers. No new flag
   sentences.
2. **Age / duration gates** on the child, over-50, and >3-week cough
   lines. Blank age shows every gated line.
3. **Deletes** — the duplicate history rows, CSO + Keeper, own commit.

Today’s forms are 19–25 items. With the wrap plus the lists above,
every condition is 9 or 10 spoken turns. Headache and cough can be 9.
