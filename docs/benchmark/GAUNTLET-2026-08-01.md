# The Gauntlet: Medicus Suite vs the UK GP add-on and AI market (run 3)

**Run date:** 2026-08-01 · **Subject:** Medicus Suite v3.208.1 · **Previous runs:** [2026-06-11](GAUNTLET-2026-06-11.md) (v3.55.0, add-on market) · [2026-07-07](GAUNTLET-2026-07-07.md) (v3.160.0, vs full EHRs)
**Framing (Phase 1):** ideas + GP appeal. Stay-sharp benchmarking, but new-feature ideas scored by how attractive they would be to GPs beyond our own practice (pre-commercial lens, not procurement).
**Comparables:** refresh of the add-on seven (Ardens, PCIT, Eclipse, FDB, APEX, GP Automate, Accurx) plus the AI wave (Anima, Klinik, Patchs, Rapid Health, eConsult, Visiba) and the scribe field (Tortus, Heidi, Accurx Scribe, Dragon Copilot, Lyrebird).
**Dimensions ranked:** (1) wow-factor, the thing that makes a GP say "I want that tomorrow" in a demo, (2) clinical safety depth, (3) feature depth.
**Method:** four research agents over primary sources (vendor docs/KBs, help centres, G-Cloud, NHS/ICB documents, MHRA/NHSE policy, trial registries, peer-reviewed studies). Vendor sites again uniformly 403'd direct fetches through the sandbox proxy; every competitor claim rests on search-indexed extracts of the cited primary pages, is labelled shipped/partial/claimed/absent, and is date-stamped 2026-08-01. The suite is scored from its own code by the same standard: roadmap = absent. **Process note:** this run initially scored the suite from a stale v3.125.0 checkout; the self-baseline below was re-verified against main at v3.208.1 before publication, and the candidate-feature verdict was re-run against the current tree.

## Executive summary

Our position: the suite is no longer a read-only overlay. Since the July run it crossed its own Rubicon into explicit, user-initiated writes through Medicus's own controls (lab-result filing, appointment booking, task creation, prescription re-assignment, document filing, problem-list edits), while keeping the fail-closed, kill-switched, CSO-gated posture. It remains the only product on any UK GP platform combining per-patient safety alerting, free-text request triage, severity-triaged results filing, signing-moment monitoring context, reception decision support, live practice-pressure ops and a personal safety-net ledger in one tool, and it is still the only deep in-record intelligence layer on Medicus. The strategic weather is unchanged from July but louder: **Doctolib owns the host** (May 2026, £100M UK plan, a stated ambition to automate 50% of routine secretarial tasks by end-2026), the Medicus estate is scaling from ~10 live practices towards 97 across 18 ICBs, and the window for any given feature is 12 to 24 months before the platform can absorb it.

**Three most dangerous competitors:** Doctolib/Medicus itself (native Consultation Assistant live in France, Aaron.ai phone AI in Germany, nothing UK-shipped yet, all claimed; frenemy, not just threat); Anima (the only shipped LLM agent doing consultation notes plus inbound letter summarise/code/task on EMIS/S1, exactly the modality we have only started); Ardens (Manager dashboards already live on Medicus via IM1, Ardens Clinical in-record templates now in phased testing, HTN award for the SEL ICB deployment).

**Single biggest gap:** still the **letters modality**, but the gap narrowed honestly: "Save attachment as document" (v3.163 onwards) ships one-click filing of triage attachments with a SNOMED-typed quick-pick, which is the plumbing; what is still absent is reading the letter, the started/stopped/changed medication diff, the 2WW-outcome flag, the action-phrase surfacing. That remaining slice is ranked GP pain #3 (inbox/letter coding burden), only Docman AI and Anima ship against it, neither on Medicus, and the MHRA clarified on 29 July 2026 that suggesting clinical codes for clinician review is not a medical device.

**Single biggest opportunity:** the June run's two headline proposals shipped and worked (results-queue triage grew into lab auto-filing; the 2WW safety-net worklist landed in Referrals). The next equivalents, per the code-grounded verdict below: **early-request/over-use chips in the Signing Queue** (the issue-history data is already fetched and currently discarded by the normaliser: highest wow-per-hour on the sheet), the **Urgent extension of the referral safety-net** (a one-parameter change to shipped code, aimed at the 25 to 30 min/GP/day chasing figure), and **Letter Lens v1 scoped to task attachments** (the only confirmed content read path), attacking pain #3 on the shipped medication-diff and document-type machinery.

**Read on the scribe wave:** do not build one. 24 suppliers on the NHSE AVT registry, Heidi took a 1,239-practice NHS Midlands procurement, Accurx Scribe passed a million consultations, and Medicus natively lists Heidi and Tortus integrations. Scribing is saturated; the unsolved market is what happens to the record and the inbox after the consultation, which is where this suite already lives.

## Gap analysis

### Table stakes we are missing
1. **The letter-reading slice of the documents modality.** Filing and typed coding of attachments shipped; reading the content (med changes, outcomes, actions) did not. Docman AI, Anima and SystmOne's native OCR all ship against it; it is GP pain #3 and was the July panel's top clinical ask. The plumbing built since (document-type quick-pick, attachment chips) cuts the remaining effort materially.
2. **External clinical-safety assurance** (DCB0129 assessment, DTAC, DSPT). Carried from both prior runs, unmoved, and the framing shift to "GP appeal beyond our practice" makes it the first question any second practice asks. The artefacts exist and are unusually good; the assurance does not. Process, not code.
3. **QOF whole-register position and £ projection.** Native in both incumbent EHRs, solved by Ardens Manager at 18p/patient/yr; our per-patient QOF awareness still cannot answer "will we hit the threshold by March".
4. **Early-request / over-use flags at signing.** EMIS ships natively; our signing surface is otherwise the field's best, which makes the absence conspicuous.
5. **Coded-refset precision.** Carried from June; substring matching remains the documented weak joint, mitigated (Keeper runs, term-coverage snapshots, matched-term tooltips) but not closed.

### Parity
- Normal-results automation ≈ GP Automate / SystmOne, on different platforms with different risk postures; ours is the only fail-closed-documented one.
- QOF indicator tracking ≈ Ardens/PCIT (they add contract-finance framing).
- Ops dashboards: ahead of APEX on liveness, behind nobody on Medicus; APEX static since 2022.
- Request triage function ≈ Patchs AI/Rapid Health at intake; ours sits on the worked queue, theirs at submission. Different moments, comparable value.

### White space (verified across the full field, 2026-08-01)
1. **Free-text red-flag triage of inbound request text.** Still unique. Accurx's entire AI inbox is still a register-interest page; Patchs is the only genuine free-text ML alternative, enabled in ~20% of its estate.
2. **Results-queue triage with under-prioritised detection.** Ours alone on any platform.
3. **The signing moment.** Monitoring currency, renal context, prescribing-safety warnings at the point of authorising a repeat: nobody else, on any platform (July run verdict, still standing).
4. **Live same-minute practice-pressure dashboards.** Ardens Manager is daily-batch, APEX retrospective, EMIS-X Analytics ICB-tier.
5. **Letter intelligence on Medicus.** Nobody (Anima/Docman own it on other stacks). Partially entered via attachment filing.
6. **Safe-capacity evidence** under the imposed 2026/27 contract. Brand-new pain, zero products anywhere, and structurally off-limits to the two vendor groups best placed to build it (the platform and the triage vendors, whose product is the demand channel).
7. **Reception red-flag CDS with structured escalation** and **STOPP/START + ACB at point of care.** Both still unique.

Where a competitor is simply better on a ranked dimension: **Anima beats us on wow-factor today** (a live agent writing the note, coding the letter and messaging the patient demos better than any overlay; their platform, not ours). **Accurx and Heidi beat us on adoption motion** (bottom-up freemium; we are named-users). **Ardens beats us on compliance posture and content breadth-per-pound.** **FDB beats us on evidence base.** On Medicus, nobody beats us on anything yet; that clause has a Doctolib-shaped clock on it.

## Verdict from the practice (virtual Dave)

The candidate features were put to the virtual-dave agent twice; the first pass accidentally reviewed the stale v3.125.0 tree, so a second pass was run against v3.208.1 with the current DPIA, hazard log and intended-purpose statement. The second pass is authoritative and is folded into the plan above. Its own words, condensed:

**Build first, in order:**
1. **Early-request chips in the Signing Queue** — "the data is already in the response you already fetch and the normaliser is literally throwing it away" (`engine/normalisers.js` keeps `dates[0]` of the full issue history and discards the rest; dosage and quantity ride the same payload). Highest wow-per-hour, no new network surface, no new identity risk. The hard thinking is what "can't parse the dose" renders as — and it must render as "can't tell", sorted up, never as fine. A good first substantial piece of work for Nick.
2. **Referral safety-net → Urgent** — `buildSafetyNet` already accepts a `priorities` option; the caller passes none. One parameter, its own longer windows, an afternoon including tests, aimed straight at the RCGP chasing figure. It also earns the right to do the diagnostics/A&G discovery properly instead of guessing.
3. **Letter Lens v1, task attachments only** — the only confirmed content read path; the med differ (`shared/record-delta.js`) and the 1,768-member document-type refset are already shipped and tested. UNMATCHED must be the loudest element on the card; the silent-miss is the failure mode. Biggest genuine differentiator, ~12-month clock.

**Killed or downgraded:**
- **Safe-capacity evidence pack: kill in the proposed form.** The snapshot store is 90-day, panel-open-only, gap-ridden by construction and per browser profile — one workstation's view of practice demand. "Building a CQC-shaped artefact on it would be dressing up thin data in the clothes of assurance", the failure H-033 already logs. Salvage the demand-coverage honesty line.
- **QOF Runway → "points at risk in the cohort you've looked at".** The £ maths is shipped and good; the register denominator is not reachable from any endpoint the suite has, and iterating a 9,000-patient list through the per-patient API "is not a feature, it's an incident". Two irreconcilable numbers is how a partner stops trusting everything else.

**Housekeeping that bites first** (pre-conditions for shipping any of this): `docs/VISION.md` still asserts the suite "writes nothing back" while the signed intended-purpose statement enumerates eleven write paths — the same class of stale-boundary error the v3.202.0 re-freeze exists to kill, and it is the document people read first. `INTENDED-PURPOSE.md` misdescribes the Submissions feed (patient-request task counts, not QOF submission status). The hazard log numbering jumps H-052 → H-055. The Clinical Safety Notice's CORRECTION PENDING on the Transactional API blocks any architecture on that path until closed.

**On Doctolib:** QOF register position goes native first; letter processing second (but needs host-side plumbing they haven't shipped — ~12 months of room); early-request chips third or fourth; the complaint chronology never ("it's a partner's problem, not a workflow").
